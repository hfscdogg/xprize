Problem

Field service techs spend 20–30 min per day typing call summaries into the CRM. They skip half of them. Notes are inconsistent, and managers can't find anything later.

Approach

Mobile-first PWA. Tech taps once after a call, talks for 30 seconds. A Whisper + GPT-4o pipeline transcribes, classifies (work order update, follow-up needed, parts ordered, etc.) and writes a typed update back to HubSpot/Salesforce/ServiceTitan via their REST APIs.
